## Summary
Feature request (Feb 2020, lnd v0.8.2) from nicolasburtey (Galoy) asking that
"limbo balance", the funds tied up in channels that are force-closing, be
included in a balance RPC. Title says `channelbalance`, body says
`walletbalance`. On current master it is in neither: limbo balance is only
reported by `PendingChannels` as `total_limbo_balance`. No comments, no
cross-references, no maintainer response in six years. Product call: extend
one of the balance RPCs, or close as not planned because the current split is
deliberate.

## Evidence
- `ChannelBalanceResponse` (`lnrpc/lightning.proto`) has local/remote,
  unsettled, and pending-open balances. Nothing for pending-close or limbo.
- `WalletBalanceResponse` has total/confirmed/unconfirmed, locked, and
  anchor-reserve. Nothing for limbo.
- `PendingChannelsResponse.total_limbo_balance` (`lnrpc/lightning.proto:2947`)
  is populated in `rpcserver.go:4550-4555` from the force-close and
  waiting-close channel lists. That is the only place the number surfaces.
- `ChannelBalance` was redesigned in v0.11 (the fields that split local/remote
  and pending-open) and limbo was not added then either, which suggests the
  omission is a design choice rather than an oversight: limbo funds are
  neither in a live channel nor spendable from the wallet.
- No duplicate issue found for "limbo balance" as a feature request; the hits
  are unrelated stuck-force-close bug reports.

## Decision needed
Should a balance RPC include limbo funds? Options:
1. Close as not planned. The number is available from `PendingChannels`, and
   putting funds that are not yet spendable into `walletbalance` or
   `channelbalance` would make those totals misleading. A draft is below.
2. Keep open as a small enhancement: add a `pending_close_balance` field to
   `ChannelBalanceResponse`. Low effort, but it needs a maintainer to agree
   on the semantics first.

## Draft comment (only if you choose option 1)
Closing this as not planned. Limbo balance is money from channels that are
in the middle of force-closing, so it is not in a channel and not yet
spendable from the wallet. lnd keeps it separate on purpose and reports it
through the `pendingchannels` command as `total_limbo_balance`, so each
balance command only shows what it actually covers. If your application
needs a single combined figure, the right place to add the numbers up is in
the application, using the three commands lnd already provides.
